# frontend

Study existing components and visual conventions. Model loading, empty, error and success states explicitly. Use semantic controls, visible focus and readable contrast. Avoid effects for derived state. Verify keyboard operation and at least one narrow viewport. Do not populate operational screens with fabricated data.

## Execution discipline

Follow the user's current scope and AgentScope permissions. Load only supporting references relevant to this task. Use actual tool evidence, stop on an unexplained failure, and report what was verified and what remains uncertain. Never fabricate outputs or bypass denied actions.
